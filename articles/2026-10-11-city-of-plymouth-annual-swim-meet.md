# Nanu Wins Seven as Leander, Devonport and Caradon Share a Medal Haul at City of Plymouth Meet

*Plymouth — 11 October 2026*

Local clubs packed the Plymouth Life Centre for the City of Plymouth Annual Swim Meet, a Level 3 short-course gala staged on 3–4 October and completed on 11 October. Age-group medals compiled from the official individual finals and timed finals show a tight contest at the top: Plymouth Leander collected 125 medals (48 gold, 44 silver, 33 bronze) from 69 swimmers, and Mount Kelly 122 (55-31-36) from 53.

Plymouth College Aquatics finished with 27 gold, 21 silver and 16 bronze medals from 22 swimmers, and won three of the five mixed relays. Harry Stevens, 11, was the college’s leading individual winner, taking the 11-year-old 50m breaststroke (42.80), 200m freestyle (2:39.38), 200m backstroke (2:52.52), 200m individual medley (2:57.57) and 400m freestyle (5:25.88). George Ward, 12, won four events, including the 1500m freestyle in 19:47.08, and Lena Crisp, 13, also won four, among them the 800m freestyle in 10:28.77. Blake Shaw, 14, took three titles, including the 50m butterfly in 29.65. The college won the mixed 9–13 200m medley relay in 2:22.00 (Crisp, Stevens, Talia Barratt and Ward), the mixed 14 and over medley relay in 2:04.62 (James Moorcraft, Aroa Medrano, Miruna-Ioana Badea and Tobias Maximilian Nowak), and the mixed 9–13 200m freestyle relay in 2:01.11.

Plymouth Leander’s 48 individual golds were spread through the age groups. Amelia Davies, 10, and Noah Martins, 12, each won five events. Ashby Edmonds, 16, took the 100m backstroke (1:03.58), 100m individual medley (1:02.85) and 400m individual medley (4:56.18). Kyan Broc Pennington, 16, won the 200m breaststroke (2:39.69), 200m freestyle (2:06.16) and 50m butterfly (28.90). Isabelle Leverton, 9, won three events, and Jessica Goodrum, 14, took the 50m breaststroke in 35.38 and the 800m freestyle in 10:01.49.

Tavistock collected six golds, five silvers and five bronzes from 16 swimmers. Charlotte Walker, 21, won the women’s 17 and over 400m freestyle in 4:48.24 and the 800m freestyle in 10:02.45. Arabella Boxall-Leggett, 14, won the 400m individual medley (5:50.70) and the 50m backstroke (33.81), and Lottie Squire, 12, won the 400m individual medley (6:24.92) and the 50m butterfly (37.61).

Devonport Royal recorded 42 gold, 25 silver and 24 bronze medals from 43 swimmers. Maria Nanu, 11, won seven events: the 50m freestyle (32.79), 50m backstroke (36.68), 50m butterfly (35.24), 200m freestyle (2:33.87), 200m butterfly (3:03.44), 200m individual medley (2:57.16) and 400m individual medley (6:13.87). Charlotte Coles, 15, and Isaac Williams, 10, each won six, Coles including the 50m breaststroke (36.46), 100m breaststroke (1:20.67) and 200m individual medley (2:42.36). Jake Jaworski, 15, won a sprint treble (50m freestyle 26.88, 50m backstroke 30.67, 50m butterfly 29.05). On the final Sunday, Devonport won the mixed 9 and over 300m freestyle relay in 2:49.81.

Caradon were close behind with 30 gold, 38 silver and 30 bronze medals from 42 swimmers, and won the mixed 14 and over 200m freestyle relay in 1:52.96. Bethany Baker, 16, won four events, including the 400m freestyle in 5:15.61 and the 1500m freestyle in 20:10.09. Noah George, 14, took three, among them the 800m freestyle in 9:36.98 and the 1500m in 18:51.14. Ellie-Louise Smethurst, 14, won the 50m freestyle (29.30) and 100m freestyle (1:04.37).

Mount Kelly, with the most individual golds of the meet, were led by Jasmine Bennett, 17, and Leopold Suffel, 13, with six wins each. Bayley Crosland, 17, won five events, including the 400m freestyle in 4:31.11, and Zhoujie Liu, 16, won four, among them the 50m freestyle in 26.04 and the 100m breaststroke in 1:10.46. Millfield did not appear in the official results.
