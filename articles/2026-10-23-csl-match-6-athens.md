# Bartee Breaks 18-Year-Old Auburn 200 Fly Record as Tigers Finish Fourth at CSL Match 6

*Athens, Ga. — Oct. 23, 2026*

Zetta Grace Bartee broke an 18-year-old Auburn record in the 200 butterfly, but Tennessee pulled away from a four-team College Swimming League field at Gabrielsen Natatorium. The Tigers scored 316 points and finished fourth behind Tennessee (668), Alabama (382) and Georgia (353), according to Auburn’s official recap.

Bartee, in only her third collegiate race in the event, won a bronze medal in 1:54.44, 0.20 under Ava Ohlgren’s school record of 1:54.64 from March 2008. The swim was an NCAA and U.S. Open cut. Tennessee’s Emily Brown won the race in 1:53.44. Taylor Bacher was fifth for Auburn in 1:57.72.

Official coverage said four Tigers landed in the Auburn record book. Gabriel Machuco again swept the medley events, winning the 100 IM in 47.14 and the 200 IM in a personal-best 1:42.96, an NCAA and U.S. Open cut. That 200 IM time, official coverage said, made him the sixth-fastest Tiger in the event and was the 17th-fastest performance in school history.

Zoey Zeller was third in the 200 breast in 2:09.21, an NCAA and U.S. Open cut that official coverage called the 17th-best time in school history, her fourth-fastest swim for the Tigers and her fifth different mark on the program’s top-25 list. Kaua Carvalho was third in the 100 breast in 52.35, which official coverage said moved him ahead of teammate Maston Ballew as the eighth-fastest Tiger in the event. Ballew was fifth in the 200 breast in 1:56.42.

Warner Russ won the men’s 50-yard skins after Auburn earned stroke choice by winning the 200 free relay. Russ was third in the opening round (19.78), second in the second (20.35), first in the third (20.19) and beat Alabama’s Toni Dragoja in the final in 20.63. Mike Rice was second in the 50 free in 19.43. Alexey Glivinsky was second in the 100 free in 42.68. Daniel Krichevsky was second in the 200 free in 1:34.30, a U.S. Open cut, and fifth in the 500 free in 4:18.74, also a U.S. Open time. Luke Bedsole tied Alabama’s Bogdan Zverev for third in the 200 fly in 1:44.23, a U.S. Open cut. Lora Komoroczy was fourth in the 100 back in 51.93, an NCAA and U.S. Open cut.

The men won the 4x50 free relay in 1:16.28 (Russ 19.03, Rice 19.13, freshman Matthew Hamilton 19.12, Nicolas Gama 19.00), their second such win in as many CSL matches. The time was under the NCAA provisional standard of 1:16.59 but outside the automatic cut of 1:16.02. Auburn’s B-relay was second in the first round of men’s super skins in 1:26.23 (Sebestyen Papp 22.14, Carvalho 23.78, Rice 21.04, Hamilton 19.27). The A-relay was disqualified in the third super-skins round on a −0.05 exchange.

Hamilton was seventh in the 100 fly in 46.89, split 19.12 on the winning 4x50 free relay, and anchored the second-place B-relay in the opening super-skins round in 19.27. SwimSwam’s box score credited him with 12 CSL points (2 individual, 4.5 relay, 5.5 super skins).
