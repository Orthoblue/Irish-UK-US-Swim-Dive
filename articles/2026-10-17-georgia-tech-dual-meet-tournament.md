# Auburn Wins Georgia Tech Dual Meet Tournament as Zeller, Bartee and Komoroczy Post Top-25 Times

*Atlanta, Ga. — Oct. 17, 2026*

Auburn swept the Georgia Tech Dual Meet Tournament at McAuley Aquatic Center, winning all three combined duals: 593–197 over George Washington on Friday, 473.5–314.5 over Kentucky later Friday, and 462–324 over the host Yellow Jackets on Saturday.

The women dominated each dual. They beat George Washington 308–88, Kentucky 245–149 and Georgia Tech 293–99. The men beat George Washington 285–109 and Kentucky 228.5–165.5, then dropped the Saturday men’s dual to Georgia Tech, 169–225, per the official Hy-Tek results. (Auburn’s meet recap reversed that men’s score; the combined 462–324 total matches a 293–99 women’s win and a 169–225 men’s loss.)

Saturday produced three program top-25 swims. Lora Komoroczy won the 100 back in 51.74, an NCAA and U.S. Open cut that official coverage called the 24th-best time in Auburn history and her ninth appearance on the event’s top-25 list. Zetta Grace Bartee won the 200 fly in 1:55.63, also an NCAA and U.S. Open cut, the 11th-fastest time and seventh-fastest performer in program history. Zoey Zeller won the 200 breast in a personal-best 2:08.45, an NCAA and U.S. Open cut that official coverage said made her the sixth-fastest Tiger in the event.

Sammie Hamilton won the 500 free against Georgia Tech in 4:44.70, an NCAA cut that missed the U.S. Open standard of 4:44.49 by 0.21. Isabella Ekk, Rosalie Reef and Isabel Iwasyk went 1-2-3 in the 100 free in 48.61, 48.93 and 49.02. Abigail Heizer, Komoroczy and Reef went 1-2-3 in the 50 free in 22.55, 22.57 and 22.65. Gabriel Machuco won the 200 IM in 1:44.15. Warner Russ won the 50 free in 19.39.

The women won the 400 medley relay in 3:34.64 (Komoroczy 51.96, Annika Finzen 1:01.19, Bartee 52.55, Ekk 48.94). They also won the 200 free relay in 1:29.83 (Heizer 22.63, Iwasyk 22.63, Ekk 22.19, Reef 22.38); Auburn’s B-relay was disqualified for an early takeoff on the third exchange. The men won the 200 free relay in 1:17.56 (Russ 19.64, Mike Rice 19.26, freshman Matthew Hamilton 19.26, Nicolas Gama 19.40). Georgia Tech’s Ricky Balduccini won the 100 free in 42.54, with Rice second in 43.10. The Auburn men were second in the 400 medley relay in 3:09.65 (Ivan Tarasov 48.59, Kaua Carvalho 52.74, Bradford Johnson 45.70, Russ 42.62).

Juliette Landi won both women’s boards against Georgia Tech (3-meter 170.15, 1-meter 140.10), and Chloe Brothers won the 1-meter (153.15). Peder Hubred was second on both men’s boards (3-meter 198.75, 1-meter 190.30).

Friday’s openers were more one-sided. Against George Washington, Glivinsky, Hamilton and Daniel Krichevsky swept the 100 free in 42.99, 43.60 and 43.82. Russ won the 50 free in 19.51, and Heizer won the 50 free in 22.72. The men won the 200 medley relay in 1:26.12 (Luke Bedsole 22.01, Carvalho 23.70, Rice 20.55, Gama 19.86). Landi swept the women’s diving (3-meter 163.00, 1-meter 152.30). Against Kentucky, Komoroczy won the 100 fly in 52.10, an NCAA and U.S. Open cut, with Bartee second in 52.66. Johnson won the 100 fly in 46.23. The men won the 400 free relay in 2:50.52 (Russ 42.71, Rice 42.41, Hamilton 42.92, Glivinsky 42.48). Machuco won the 200 IM in 1:43.74, an NCAA and U.S. Open cut.

Against George Washington, the women also won the 400 free relay in 3:18.26 (Iwasyk 49.67, Heizer 49.09, Wyllo Hanson 50.19, Ekk 49.31). Against Kentucky they won it in 3:16.52, with Ekk (49.40) and Iwasyk (48.90) on the first two legs.

Hamilton was second in the 100 free against George Washington in 43.60 and split 42.55 on a 400 free relay that won in 2:52.90. Against Kentucky he split 42.92 on the winning 400 free relay, was fifth in the 100 fly in 47.97, and anchored Auburn’s B 200 medley relay in 19.16 (third, 1:25.82). Against Georgia Tech he was second in the 50 free in 19.59, third in the 100 free in 43.26, and split 19.26 on the winning 200 free relay.

The Tigers are next at College Swimming League Match 6, Oct. 23, in Athens, Ga.
