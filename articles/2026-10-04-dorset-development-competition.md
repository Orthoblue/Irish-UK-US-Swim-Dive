# Poole Lead the Medal List as Dorset Clubs Gather at Littledown

*Bournemouth — 4 October 2026*

The Dorset Development Competition, a Level 3 short-course meet organised by Dorset County ASA, was held at Littledown on 3–4 October. None of Plymouth College Aquatics, Plymouth Leander, Tavistock, Devonport Royal, Caradon, Millfield or Mount Kelly appear in the official results. The field was made up of Dorset clubs.

Age-group medals compiled from the official timed finals were led by Poole Swimming Club, with 55 gold, 65 silver and 41 bronze (161) from 40 swimmers. Swim Bournemouth recorded 44-31-21 (96) from 31 swimmers, and Seagulls 31-13-21 (65) from 21. Weymouth (57), Bournemouth Collegiate School (51), West Dorset (48) and Wareham (44) also featured strongly.

Chloe Tse, 15, of Seagulls won six events, including the 50m butterfly in 33.26, the 100m freestyle in 1:06.94, the 200m freestyle in 2:28.31 and the 200m individual medley in 2:47.65. Ivan Machulin, 9, of Poole also won six, among them the 100m freestyle in 1:37.27 and the 100m individual medley in 1:48.76. Amelia Fancini, 17, of Swim Bournemouth, Freya Lyle, 10, and Reuben Hammersley, 10, of Bournemouth Collegiate School, Joshua Richards, 11, of Poole, and Katie Knight, 16, of North Dorset Turbos, each won five events. Knight’s titles included the 50m butterfly in 33.31, the 100m freestyle in 1:03.79 and the 200m individual medley in 2:39.14.
