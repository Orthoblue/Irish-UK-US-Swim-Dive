# Registe Finishes Fourth in the 50 Free as Lisburn Swimmers Compete at BUCS Short Course Championships

*Sheffield, England — Nov. 22, 2026*

Lisburn City Swimming Club sprinters competing for their universities featured at the BUCS Short Course Championships, Nov. 20–22 at Ponds Forge in Sheffield. Dylan Registe, swimming for Ulster, placed fourth in the open 50 freestyle, and Adam Colgan reached the 200 free B-final. Alfie Shawe and Alan Vaughan raced for Queen’s.

Registe, 21, qualified third for the 50 free A-final in 22.14, then finished fourth in 22.12. Loughborough’s Maddox Roberts won in 21.45, with teammate Jordan Cooley second in 21.70 and Stirling’s Thomas Leggett third in 22.00. Jensen Norris of Stirling was fifth in 22.18. Registe also made the 50 fly A-final, qualifying eighth in 23.92 and placing sixth in 23.71.

Colgan, 21, also for Ulster, reached the open 200 free B-final from 18th in 1:50.99 (25.16, 52.74, 1:21.25) and was fifth in that final in 1:50.27. He was 23rd in the 100 free in 50.78 after winning a swim-off in 49.91, 56th in the 100 IM in 1:01.52 and 99th in the 50 free in 24.17.

Christopher Mason, 21, swimming for Ulster, was 70th in the 100 free in 52.63 and 77th in the 50 free in 23.96. Ulster’s 200 freestyle relay of Registe (22.03), Mason (23.12), Daniel Edgar (24.36) and Colgan (22.84) qualified 15th in 1:34.22 and was fourth in the B-final in 1:32.35.

Shawe, 19, competing for Queen’s, was 72nd in the 50 free in 23.91, 83rd in the 100 free in 53.21 and 98th in the 50 fly in 26.62. Queen’s qualified 10th for the 200 free relay A-final in 1:33.33 and placed 10th in the final in 1:32.02, with Shawe leading off in 23.74 and Vaughan splitting 23.50.
