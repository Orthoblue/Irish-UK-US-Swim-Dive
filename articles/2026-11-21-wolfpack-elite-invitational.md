# Zeller, Bartee and Komoroczy Break Auburn Records as Tigers Finish 2nd and 3rd at Wolfpack Elite Invitational

*Greensboro, N.C. — Nov. 21, 2026*

Auburn left the Greensboro Aquatic Center with three school records, a men’s 200 freestyle win and a pair of top-three team finishes at the Wolfpack Elite Invitational, held Nov. 18–21.

The 19th-ranked Tiger women closed within 74.5 points of host and eighth-ranked NC State, taking second with 1,701.50 points. The 14th-ranked men were third with 1,247, finishing 38.5 behind Virginia Tech and 14.5 ahead of Arizona. George Washington rounded out the five-team field.

Senior Zoey Zeller became the first Auburn woman under 2:08 in the 200 breaststroke, winning silver in a school-record 2:07.99. Freshman Zetta Grace Bartee lowered her own school record in the 200 butterfly to 1:53.79 and later added a runner-up 1:57.72 in the 200 IM, the 10th-fastest time in program history. Junior Lora Komoroczy broke Aly Tetzloff’s seven-year-old 100 backstroke record with a 50.86 leadoff split on the 400 medley relay, then came within 0.02 of that mark in the individual final.

Sophomore Daniel Krichevsky supplied Auburn’s only individual event win on the last night, taking the men’s 200 freestyle in 1:33.35. Bradford Johnson and Mike Rice went 2-3 in the 100 butterfly in 45.29 and 45.37, both program top-10 performances, and the Auburn men went 1-2 in the opening 200 medley relay in 1:22.65 and 1:22.92, the fifth- and ninth-fastest times in school history.

Freshman Matthew Hamilton, a sprint free/fly swimmer from Lisburn, Northern Ireland, made the A-final of the 100 butterfly (seventh, 46.88), posted a 43.19 in 100 freestyle prelims that missed the U.S. Open cut by 0.10, and split 18.65 and 19.33 on scoring relays. His swims are included with the rest of the roster below; every Auburn swimmer who appears in the official Hy-Tek results is covered.

Auburn’s divers were not in Greensboro. They competed the same week at the Tennessee Diving Invitational in Knoxville.

## Day 1 — Wednesday, Nov. 18

Wednesday was a timed-finals session: the 200 medley relays, the 1650 freestyle and the 800 freestyle relays.

The Auburn men opened the meet with a 1-2 finish in the 200 medley relay, both under the NCAA qualifying standard of 1:23.52. The A-relay of Luke Bedsole (21.35), Uros Zivanovic (22.89), Bradford Johnson (20.11) and Warner Russ (18.30) won in 1:22.65, the fifth-fastest time in program history. The B-relay of Ivan Tarasov (21.12), Kaua Carvalho (23.18), Mike Rice (19.97) and Matthew Hamilton (18.65) was second in 1:22.92, the ninth-fastest time in Auburn history. An exhibition C-relay of Sebestyen Papp (21.63), Arsen Kozhakhmetov (23.21), Mack Schumann (20.84) and Nicolas Gama (19.14) went 1:24.82.

The Auburn women were third in the 200 medley relay in 1:36.80 with Lora Komoroczy (23.46), Kiia Metsakonkola (27.96), Isabel “Izzy” Iwasyk (23.26) and Rosalie Reef (22.12). The time was less than a second from Auburn’s program top-10 and missed the NCAA provisional cut of 1:36.36. The B-relay of Nika Sharafutdinova (25.03), Annika Finzen (27.47), Ellis Fox (23.32) and Abigail Heizer (22.29) was sixth in 1:38.11. An exhibition C-relay of Valeriia Egorova (24.98), Zoey Zeller (27.20), Morgan Carteaux (23.65) and Hanna Schmidt (23.25) went 1:39.08.

In the 1650 freestyle, Sammie Hamilton was eighth in 16:54.93 and Nora Weber ninth in 16:56.13. Neither time reached the NCAA (16:34.79) or U.S. Open (16:24.29) cuts. Mack Schumann was entered in the men’s 1650 and was listed as a declared false start; no other Auburn man appears in that event.

The women’s 800 freestyle relay took second in 7:08.01 with Bella Ekk (1:46.63), Maggie McGuire (1:47.21), Wyllo Hanson (1:47.91) and Zetta Grace Bartee (1:46.26). The B-relay of Lilly Gault Abdella (1:47.63), Sammie Hamilton (1:48.05), Taylor Bacher (1:49.44) and Carissa Rinard (1:51.90) was fifth in 7:17.02. Neither relay reached the NCAA provisional cut of 7:04.46.

The men’s 800 freestyle relay was third in 6:15.76, 0.17 off the 10th-fastest time in school history and just 0.04 under the NCAA provisional cut of 6:15.80. The splits: Daniel Krichevsky 1:33.72, Bedsole 1:33.47, Gabriel Machuco 1:33.74 and Alexey Glivinsky 1:34.83. (The Hy-Tek file spells Glivinsky as “Glivinskiy”; the official Auburn roster uses Glivinsky.)

## Day 2 — Thursday, Nov. 19

Thursday produced two school records and Komoroczy’s first individual win of the meet.

Komoroczy won the women’s 200 backstroke in a personal-best 1:53.52, the 10th-fastest time in Auburn history and under both the NCAA (1:55.16) and U.S. Open (1:55.39) cuts, after a 1:54.63 prelim. Fox was sixth in 1:56.92 (prelim 1:56.72). McGuire took 12th in the B-final in 1:58.14 (prelim 1:57.57, ninth). Finzen was 13th in 1:59.04 (prelim 1:59.84). Sharafutdinova qualified 12th in prelims in 1:58.65 but does not appear in the final results.

Tarasov was Auburn’s only men’s 200 backstroker, placing fifth in 1:42.03 (prelim 1:43.37), a U.S. Open cut (1:43.29) but outside the NCAA cut of 1:41.11.

Reef led the women’s 100 freestyle in fifth at 48.72 (prelim 48.75, tied for fourth), a U.S. Open cut (48.99). Iwasyk dropped to 48.79 for ninth in the B-final (prelim 49.47). Ekk was 11th in 49.02, Hanson 18th in 49.77, Abdella 20th in 50.15, Heizer 24th in 50.26 and Metsakonkola 29th in 51.10.

Rice led the Auburn men in the 100 freestyle, going 42.15 for fifth (prelim 42.52) and clearing the NCAA cut of 42.33. Russ was seventh in 42.26 (prelim 42.28). Glivinsky had the fastest Tiger prelim, 42.18, then finished eighth in 43.08; the prelim stands as an NCAA and U.S. Open cut. Krichevsky won the B-final in 42.36 for ninth. Machuco was 13th in 43.05 (prelim 43.03). Hamilton was 15th in 43.45 after a 43.19 prelim that missed the U.S. Open cut of 43.09 by 0.10. Gama was 16th in 43.63 (prelim 43.42). Papp closed the C-final in 42.77 for 17th, a U.S. Open cut.

Zeller then rewrote the record book. She went 2:08.66 in 200 breaststroke prelims — already her sixth appearance on Auburn’s top-25 list in the event — and 2:07.99 in the final, the first sub-2:08 in school history, a runner-up finish, and both an NCAA (2:11.08) and U.S. Open (2:11.99) cut. Schmidt was sixth in 2:13.22 (prelim 2:13.19). Olivia Dinehart was seventh in 2:13.62. Audrey Crawford placed 12th in the B-final in 2:15.31 (prelim 2:17.64).

On the men’s side, Carvalho led prelims of the 200 breaststroke in 1:53.93, the 24th-best time and sixth-fastest performer in Auburn history, then placed fifth in 1:54.59. The prelim is an NCAA (1:54.08) and U.S. Open cut. Maston Ballew took the bronze in 1:53.99 (prelim 1:54.11), also an NCAA and U.S. Open cut. Kozhakhmetov was 10th in 1:58.23. Zivanovic qualified 11th in 1:58.28 but does not appear in the B-final.

Bartee won the women’s 200 butterfly and lowered her own school record by 0.65 seconds, touching in 1:53.79 after a 1:55.78 prelim that official Auburn coverage called the 20th-fastest time in program history. The final is an NCAA (1:57.38) and U.S. Open (1:57.49) cut. Coco Croxford was fifth in 1:58.59 (prelim 1:58.77). Bacher was seventh in 1:59.81 (prelim 1:58.34). Carteaux was eighth in 2:00.41. Rinard took 11th in the B-final in 2:01.61.

Bedsole led Auburn in the men’s 200 butterfly, placing fifth in 1:43.25 (prelim 1:44.02), a U.S. Open cut (1:44.79) just outside the NCAA cut of 1:43.10. Johnson was ninth in 1:44.19 (prelim 1:45.73), also a U.S. Open cut.

The women’s 400 freestyle relay was third in 3:16.03 with Iwasyk (49.36), Komoroczy (49.01), Reef (48.85) and Ekk (48.81). The B-relay of Hanson (50.02), Heizer (49.83), Abdella (49.97) and Bartee (50.18) was sixth in 3:20.00. Both missed the NCAA provisional cut of 3:14.63.

The men closed the night with the sixth-fastest 400 freestyle relay in program history — and an NCAA qualifying time. Russ (42.35), Rice (41.38), Glivinsky (41.99) and Krichevsky (42.51) went 2:48.23, under the 2:48.73 NCAA standard, for second. The B-relay of Machuco (43.14), Hamilton (43.46), Papp (42.47) and Gama (42.93) was fifth in 2:52.00.

## Day 3 — Friday, Nov. 20

Komoroczy’s 50.86 leadoff on the 400 medley relay was the headline Friday: a school record in the 100 backstroke, 0.06 under Aly Tetzloff’s mark of 50.92. Official Auburn coverage noted that Komoroczy is only the second Tiger ever under 51 seconds in the event.

The relay itself was second in 3:31.57, an NCAA provisional time (standard 3:32.05) that missed the automatic qualifying cut of 3:30.66 by 0.91. After Komoroczy’s 50.86, Zeller split 1:00.07, Bartee 52.65 and Reef 47.99. The B-relay of Fox (52.70), Finzen (1:00.42), Iwasyk (53.50) and Ekk (48.65) was fifth in 3:35.27. Exhibition C- and D-relays went 3:37.65 (Sharafutdinova, Dinehart, Carteaux, Hanson) and 3:38.82 (Egorova, Metsakonkola, Rinard, Heizer).

The men’s 400 medley A-relay of Tarasov (46.30), Carvalho (52.26), Johnson (45.34) and Rice (41.51) was also second, in 3:05.41, 0.16 outside the NCAA provisional cut of 3:05.25. The B-relay was disqualified; Hy-Tek lists a −0.07 exchange on the second takeoff (Bedsole, Kozhakhmetov, Hamilton, Russ). An exhibition C-relay of Papp (46.79), Ballew (52.56), Machuco (46.43) and Gama (42.92) went 3:08.70.

Sammie Hamilton was the top Auburn woman in the 500 freestyle, moving from a 4:50.12 prelim to 4:44.07 and second place. SwimSwam’s day-three recap said the swim was 0.43 off her lifetime best. The time is both an NCAA cut (4:44.93) and a U.S. Open cut (4:44.49). Abdella was sixth in 4:52.34 (prelim 4:51.68). McGuire was 12th in 4:50.90. Weber was 20th in 4:54.09. Croxford recorded 5:00.64 in prelims (23rd, with a qualifying mark in the results) and does not appear in a final. Bartee was listed as a declared false start in the 500.

Schumann led the Auburn men in the 500, taking third in 4:18.37 (prelim 4:20.78), a U.S. Open cut (4:19.89). Krichevsky was seventh in 4:22.53 (prelim 4:21.08).

Bartee and Zeller then went 2-3 in the women’s 200 IM. Bartee’s 1:57.72 (prelim 2:01.14) is the 10th-fastest time in Auburn history and clears both the NCAA (1:58.66) and U.S. Open (1:58.39) cuts — putting her on two program top-10 lists after the 200 fly record a day earlier. Zeller’s 1:58.89 final (third) came after a 1:58.53 prelim that is an NCAA cut. Bacher was fifth in 1:59.90. Finzen (2:00.59, 10th), Crawford (2:02.21, 11th), Dinehart (2:03.25, 13th) and Schmidt (2:03.41, 15th) scored in the B-final.

Machuco nearly won the men’s 200 IM, placing second in 1:43.72 after topping prelims in 1:43.55. Both times are NCAA (1:43.99) and U.S. Open cuts. Ballew was fifth in 1:45.38 (prelim 1:46.48), a U.S. Open cut by 0.01 (standard 1:45.39). Glivinsky was 16th in 1:51.76 (prelim 1:48.59).

In the 50 freestyle, Russ broke 19 seconds for the first time. Official Auburn coverage called the 18.90 a personal best and said it made him the ninth-fastest Tiger in the event, one spot up from 10th after his 19.03 relay leadoff at CSL Match 6. The swim is an NCAA (19.22) and U.S. Open (19.69) cut; he had gone 19.09 in prelims. Rice was seventh in 19.29 (prelim 19.38), a U.S. Open cut. Gama was 13th in 19.61 (prelim 19.83). Hamilton made the U.S. Open cut in prelims with 19.67, then placed 15th in the B-final in 19.87. Papp qualified 18th in 19.89 and does not appear in a final. Bedsole and Zivanovic were listed as declared false starts.

Reef led the Auburn women in the 50 freestyle A-final, placing seventh in 22.65 (prelim 22.54). Ekk was eighth in 22.75 (prelim 22.55). Iwasyk (22.36, ninth) and Heizer (22.45, 11th) both made the U.S. Open cut of 22.49 in the B-final. Metsakonkola tied for 13th in 22.76 (13.5 points). Hanson was 18th in 23.00. Carteaux (23.35, 26th) and Rinard (24.48, 34th) swam the D-final. Komoroczy qualified seventh in prelims in 22.53 but does not appear in the A-final. Fox qualified 20th in 23.16 and does not appear in a final.

## Day 4 — Saturday, Nov. 21

The final night opened with a near-miss in the men’s 100 butterfly. Arizona’s Haakon Naughton won in 45.22, with Johnson second in 45.29 and Rice third in 45.37, the only other swimmers under 46. Official Auburn coverage said Johnson’s 45.29 is the ninth-best time in school history and makes him the seventh-fastest Tiger in the event. Rice had led prelims in 45.65, which official coverage called a 10th-all-time performance; his 45.37 final is the 16th-best time in program history and moved him to ninth all-time among Auburn performers. Both finals are NCAA (45.59) and U.S. Open (46.69) cuts. Bedsole was sixth in 46.45 (prelim 46.12), a U.S. Open cut. Hamilton reached the A-final from 10th in prelims (46.81) and finished seventh in 46.88, 0.12 outside the U.S. Open cut.

Komoroczy (52.09, third), Bartee (52.19, fourth) and Fox (52.40, sixth) all broke the NCAA 100 butterfly cut of 52.60 and the U.S. Open cut of 52.79. Official coverage said Bartee’s 52.19 made her the 10th-fastest Tiger in the event. Carteaux was 10th in 52.70, a U.S. Open cut. Iwasyk (53.10, 11th) and Rinard (53.44, 14th) scored in the B-final, with Heizer (53.81, 17th), Bacher (54.28, 18th) and Croxford (54.43, 19th) in the C-final.

Schumann, the top seed in the men’s 400 IM after a 3:45.77 prelim, placed third in 3:45.33, a U.S. Open cut (3:47.69) outside the NCAA cut of 3:44.39. Ballew was fourth in 3:48.28 (prelim 3:53.08). Crawford was Auburn’s only women’s 400 IM finalist, placing seventh in 4:21.92 (prelim 4:24.58). Croxford qualified 12th in 4:35.69 and does not appear in the B-final.

Krichevsky then won the men’s 200 freestyle in 1:33.35, the 22nd-best time in program history and his fifth different mark on Auburn’s top-25 list in the event. The swim missed the NCAA cut of 1:33.29 by 0.06 and is a U.S. Open cut (1:34.99). Machuco was second in 1:34.57 (prelim 1:34.93), also a U.S. Open cut. Russ qualified fourth in prelims in 1:34.47, a U.S. Open cut, and does not appear in the A-final. Glivinsky was listed as a declared false start.

Ekk was third in the women’s 200 freestyle in 1:46.51 (prelim 1:47.32). Abdella was fourth in 1:46.61 after leading the Auburn prelims in 1:46.24. Hanson was seventh in 1:48.40. Bacher (1:48.44, 11th) and McGuire (1:49.17, 13th) scored in the B-final. Reef qualified seventh in 1:48.62 and does not appear in the A-final.

Zeller was second in the 100 breaststroke in 59.87 (prelim 1:00.18), an NCAA (1:00.41) and U.S. Open (1:00.79) cut. Finzen was seventh in 1:00.45, a U.S. Open cut. Dinehart (1:00.89, 10th), Schmidt (1:02.21, 12th) and Metsakonkola (1:02.93, 14th) scored in the B-final.

Carvalho topped 100 breaststroke prelims in a personal-best 52.11, which official coverage said made him the seventh-fastest Tiger in the event, then placed third in 52.18. Both times meet the NCAA cut of 52.18 (the final equals it exactly) and the U.S. Open cut of 53.29. Kozhakhmetov was fourth in 52.51 (prelim 52.82). Ballew was sixth in 53.19 (prelim 53.53), a U.S. Open cut. Zivanovic was listed as a declared false start.

Komoroczy was second in the 100 backstroke in 50.88 (prelim 51.32), 0.02 off the school record she had set the night before, and well under the NCAA (52.59) and U.S. Open (52.99) cuts. Fox tied for sixth in 53.01 (23.5 points). Egorova was 10th in 53.31. Sharafutdinova tied for 12th in 54.06 (14.5 points). McGuire (55.29, 19th) and Rinard (55.33, 20th) scored in the C-final.

Tarasov was third in the men’s 100 backstroke in 45.73 (prelim 45.99), an NCAA (45.84) and U.S. Open (46.99) cut. Papp was seventh in 46.91 (prelim 46.78), a U.S. Open cut. Bedsole was listed as a declared false start.

The men’s 200 freestyle relay was second in 1:16.13, an NCAA provisional time that missed the automatic cut of 1:16.02 by 0.11. The splits: Russ 19.06, Rice 18.95, Hamilton 19.33 and Bedsole 18.79. The B-relay of Gama (19.58), Papp (19.72), Krichevsky (19.82) and Machuco (19.58) was sixth in 1:18.70. An exhibition C-relay of Tarasov (20.29), Ballew (19.92), Carvalho (20.85) and Johnson (20.59) went 1:21.65.

The women’s 200 freestyle A-relay of Iwasyk (22.79), Komoroczy (21.85), Reef (22.07) and Heizer (22.30) finished third in 1:29.01. (Auburn’s official day-four recap called this a second-place finish; the official Hy-Tek results list it third, and the 54-point award matches third place.) The time missed the NCAA provisional cut of 1:28.54. The B-relay of Ekk (22.65), Hanson (23.02), Abdella (23.20) and Metsakonkola (22.63) was seventh in 1:31.50. An exhibition C-relay of Bartee (23.37), Fox (22.49), Carteaux (22.98) and Schmidt (23.28) went 1:32.12. An exhibition D-relay was disqualified (Sharafutdinova, Egorova, Rinard, Dinehart), with Hy-Tek listing a −0.12 exchange on the second takeoff.
