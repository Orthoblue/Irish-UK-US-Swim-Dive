# Machuco Sweeps the IM as Auburn Takes Third at CSL Match 3

*Stanford, Calif. — Oct. 2, 2026*

Auburn opened its College Swimming League schedule with a third-place finish at Match 3, scoring 371.5 points at Avery Aquatic Center behind Stanford (550) and California (543.5). Ohio State was fourth with 255. The result was worth two league points.

Freshman Gabriel Machuco won both individual medleys in his collegiate debut, and the men took the 4x50 freestyle relay. Warner Russ and Mike Rice went 1-2 in the 50 free.

Machuco won the 100 IM in 47.39 and the 200 IM in 1:43.32, an NCAA and U.S. Open cut. Official coverage said the 200 IM time tied Joe Patching for the 25th-best performance in Auburn history. Alexey Glivinsky was third in the 100 IM in 48.80.

Russ won the 50 free in 19.21, an NCAA and U.S. Open cut, with Rice second in 19.47. Earlier, Russ, Rice, Nicolas Gama and freshman Matthew Hamilton — a sprinter from Lisburn, Northern Ireland, who represented Northern Ireland at the 2026 Commonwealth Games and Ireland at the European Aquatics Championships in August — won the men’s 4x50 free relay in 1:16.72 (Russ 19.13, Gama 19.77, Rice 18.87, Hamilton 18.95). The time was outside the NCAA automatic (1:16.02) and provisional (1:16.59) standards.

Rice was second in the 100 free in 42.95, and Daniel Krichevsky was third in 43.11. Krichevsky also took third in the 200 free in 1:33.72, a U.S. Open cut, and was fourth in the 500 free in 4:19.47, another U.S. Open time. Bradford Johnson was second in the 100 fly in 46.28. Kaua Carvalho was third in the 100 breast in 53.24. Luke Bedsole was fourth in the 200 fly in 1:44.46, a U.S. Open cut. Ivan Tarasov was fourth in the 100 back (47.25) and sixth in the 200 back (1:43.85).

In the men’s 50-yard skins, Uros Zivanovic won the opening round in 23.87 and Carvalho was second in 23.90. Carvalho was third in the next two rounds (24.32, 24.69) and, official coverage said, scored 19 points; Zivanovic was fourth in those heats (24.47, 25.10) and scored 18. California’s Yamato Okadome won the later rounds.

Sammie Hamilton was third in the 500 free in 4:43.64, an NCAA and U.S. Open cut that official coverage called her first podium of the year. Lora Komoroczy was second in the 100 back in 52.09, an NCAA and U.S. Open cut — official results placed her second behind California’s Mary-Ambre Moluh (51.40). Zoey Zeller was fourth in the 200 breast in 2:10.14, an NCAA and U.S. Open cut. Zetta Grace Bartee was third in the 100 fly in 52.68 in her collegiate debut, a U.S. Open cut that missed the NCAA standard of 52.60 by 0.08, and fourth in the 200 fly in 1:57.54. Isabella Ekk was third in the 100 free in 49.07. Ellis Fox was fifth in the 200 back in 1:56.14, and Nika Sharafutdinova was seventh in 1:56.29. The women’s 4x50 free relay was fourth in 1:29.40 (Komoroczy 22.77, Isabel Iwasyk 22.08, Rosalie Reef 22.38, Ekk 22.17).

Hamilton was sixth in the 100 fly in 46.95 and anchored the winning 4x50 free relay in 18.95. He swam fly on Auburn’s B-relay in the men’s super skins, which placed fourth in the opening round in 1:26.62 (Ivan Tarasov 21.99, Carvalho 24.11, Hamilton 20.73, Rice 19.79) and third in the next two rounds. SwimSwam’s box score credited him with 16.5 CSL points (3 individual, 5 relay, 8.5 super skins).
