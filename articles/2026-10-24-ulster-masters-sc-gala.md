# Alexis Jacquemin Wins Three 18–24 Events as Lisburn Masters Medal at Ulster Masters SC Gala

*Newry, Northern Ireland — Oct. 24, 2026*

Lisburn City Swimming Club’s masters swimmers left Newry Leisure Centre with four age-group wins and two silvers at the Ulster Masters SC Gala on Oct. 24. Alexis Jacquemin, 21, won all three of the men’s 18–24 events he finished, and Jerome Jacquemin, 51, won the 50–54 200 breaststroke.

Alexis won the 18–24 100 backstroke in 1:08.56, the 18–24 100 breaststroke in 1:14.44 and the 18–24 100 IM in 1:07.68. He was entered in the 18–24 100 free but did not start.

Jerome won the 50–54 200 breaststroke in 3:27.62. He was second in the 50–54 100 breaststroke in 1:38.16, behind Leander’s Graeme Beggs (1:19.47), and second in the 50–54 100 IM in 1:38.58, again behind Beggs (1:13.71). Official results list no other Lisburn swimmers at the gala.
