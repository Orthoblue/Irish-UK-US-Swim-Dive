# Clark Wins 11 Events as Plymouth College Leads the Way at Lindsay Powell Memorial

*Plymouth — 18 October 2026*

Plymouth College Aquatics led the local clubs at the Exeter City Lindsay Powell Memorial, a Level 2 short-course meet held at Plymouth Life Centre on 17–18 October and published in the official results as the Exeter October Level 2 Meet. Compiled from the official timed finals, the college recorded 32 gold, 24 silver and 24 bronze medals from 22 swimmers.

Diego Clark, 11, was the meet’s outstanding individual, winning 11 age-group events: the 50m freestyle (29.60), 100m freestyle (1:05.90), 200m freestyle (2:24.15), 400m freestyle (5:03.73), 50m backstroke (35.54), 200m backstroke (2:47.64), 50m breaststroke (39.52), 100m breaststroke (1:26.46), 200m breaststroke (3:08.06), 100m individual medley (1:18.11) and 200m individual medley (2:43.55). Lexi Mulcahy, 15, won four, the 50m freestyle (27.72), 50m backstroke (30.17), 100m backstroke (1:04.07) and 200m backstroke (2:19.30). Ramon Luaces Chan, 14, also took four, including the 50m breaststroke in 31.25, the 100m breaststroke in 1:07.58 and the 200m individual medley in 2:21.03. Toby Nakamura-Drew, 17, won the 200m freestyle in 1:52.94 and the 200m individual medley in 2:04.49. James Moorcraft, 14, took the 400m freestyle (4:28.61) and 400m individual medley (5:09.08).

Plymouth Leander collected 18 gold, 25 silver and 23 bronze medals from 37 swimmers. William Higgs, 13, won six events, including the 50m butterfly in 27.86, the 100m freestyle in 57.06, the 200m freestyle in 2:04.65 and the 400m freestyle in 4:27.42. Jessica Goodrum, 14, won four, among them the 100m breaststroke in 1:16.20 and the 400m individual medley in 5:16.35. Isla Baggott, 15, took the 50m butterfly (28.94), 100m freestyle (59.79) and 100m individual medley (1:07.00). Ashby Edmonds, 16, won the 100m freestyle in 53.60, and Michal Lyjak, 18, the 200m backstroke in 2:13.55.

Tavistock’s eight swimmers had a quieter weekend, with a single age-group medal: Jacob Cacchione, 26, was third in the 50m backstroke in 28.73. Evie Palmer, 17, placed fourth in the 50m breaststroke in 34.88, and Arabella Boxall-Leggett, 14, was fourth in the 200m backstroke in 2:37.42.

Devonport Royal recorded 10 gold, 12 silver and 8 bronze medals from 14 swimmers. Aiden Symons-Brown, 22, won four senior sprints: the 50m freestyle in 22.02, the 100m freestyle in 49.16, the 50m backstroke in 24.82 and the 100m backstroke in 55.55. Abigail Daly, listed as S14 in the official results and aged 21, won the girls’ multi-class 100m freestyle (1:17.05), 100m butterfly (1:43.35) and 200m freestyle (2:51.61). Maria Nanu, 11, added two more titles, the 50m backstroke in 36.45 and the 200m backstroke in 2:50.91, and Tomas Crussell, 13, won the 200m butterfly in 2:34.18.

Caradon finished with 16 gold, 7 silver and 12 bronze medals from 21 swimmers. Zoe Cawsey, 27, won seven events: the 50m freestyle (26.39), 100m freestyle (58.10), 50m backstroke (28.54), 50m breaststroke (31.79), 100m breaststroke (1:10.38), 50m butterfly (27.62) and 100m individual medley (1:03.17). Megan Daniel, 16, won five, including the 50m backstroke (29.35), 100m backstroke (1:03.54), 200m backstroke (2:18.22) and 100m butterfly (1:03.63). Anna Sneddon, 16, won the 400m individual medley in 5:40.73.

Millfield did not appear in the official results. Mount Kelly had a small entry of six swimmers and recorded four golds, three silvers and six bronzes. Flavia Tinchi, 13, won the 100m and 200m breaststroke in 1:23.46 and 2:57.79, and Olivia Thorpe, 12, took the 100m and 200m backstroke in 1:11.36 and 2:32.59.
