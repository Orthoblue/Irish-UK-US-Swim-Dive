# Mulcahy Wins Six Titles as Symons-Brown, Baggott and Cawsey Strike Gold at South West Winter Championships

*Street, Somerset — 8 November 2026*

South West clubs left Millfield School with a stack of age-group titles after the Swim England South West Winter Championships, a Level 2 short-course meet held from 6–8 November and used as a qualifying opportunity for the Swim England National Winter Championships.

Plymouth College Aquatics led the Plymouth and Cornwall clubs in the official results, with 9 gold, 8 silver and 5 bronze medals in finals and timed finals. Lexi Mulcahy, 15, was the standout, winning six girls’ 13–15 events: the 50m backstroke in 28.49, the 100m backstroke in 1:00.99, the 200m backstroke in 2:13.75, the 200m individual medley in 2:19.15, the 100m freestyle in 57.98 and the 400m freestyle in 4:23.77. She added silver in the 50m freestyle (26.85) and the 100m butterfly (1:03.21).

Toby Nakamura-Drew, 17, won the men’s 16 and over 100m backstroke in 54.50 and took silver in the 50m backstroke (25.52) and the 200m individual medley (2:04.74). Tomas Stanimirov, 14, won the boys’ 13–15 50m backstroke in 26.19, was second in the 100m backstroke (57.11) and took bronze in the 50m butterfly (25.74), 100m butterfly (58.71) and 200m backstroke (2:09.71). Julius Ennals, 13, won the boys’ 13–14 400m individual medley in 5:02.52 and was second in the 800m freestyle in 9:16.65. Nathan Broadwater added silver in the 13–15 100m individual medley (1:01.06) and bronze in the 200m butterfly (2:13.10), Ethan Kaatrud was second in the 16 and over 200m butterfly (2:05.69), and Felix Escudero took bronze in the 16 and over 50m breaststroke (29.23).

Plymouth Leander collected 3 gold, 4 silver and 2 bronze medals. Isla Baggott, 15, won the 15-year-old 400m individual medley in 5:01.92, the 13–15 50m butterfly in 28.80 and the 13–15 200m freestyle in 2:07.17, and was second in the 200m individual medley in 2:22.49. Ava Edwards, 15, was second in the 13–15 50m backstroke (30.49) and 100m backstroke (1:04.79), and third in the 200m backstroke (2:21.23) and the 15-year-old 400m individual medley (5:15.64). Ashby Edmonds, 16, took silver in the 16-year-old 1500m freestyle in 16:46.59 and placed fourth in the 16 and over 800m freestyle in 8:39.51. Jessica Goodrum, 14, was fifth in the 13–14 400m individual medley (5:19.54) and fifth in the 13–15 200m breaststroke (2:42.64).

Devonport Royal’s four-strong team produced the sprint story of the weekend. Aiden Symons-Brown, 22, won the men’s 16 and over 50m freestyle in 22.13 and the 50m backstroke in 25.42. Reid Jones, 24, completed a club one-two in that 50m freestyle final in 22.86 and was eighth in the 100m freestyle (54.78). Clive Marquis, 28, of Caradon took the bronze in the same 50m freestyle final in 23.30. Xan Mitchell, 14, placed seventh in the boys’ 13–15 100m individual medley in 1:06.70. Charlotte Coles, 15, contested the 13–15 50m and 100m breaststroke heats but did not reach a final.

Caradon finished with 2 gold, 1 silver and 3 bronze medals. Zoe Cawsey, 27, won the women’s 16 and over 50m backstroke in 28.38 and the 100m breaststroke in 1:08.97, was second in the 50m breaststroke (31.84) and fourth in the 50m freestyle (26.29). Megan Daniel, 16, took bronze in the 16 and over 100m backstroke in 1:03.03 and reached five other finals, including fifth in the 200m breaststroke (2:38.32). Ellie-Louise Smethurst, 15, was third in the 13–15 50m butterfly in 29.38, behind Baggott.

Tavistock’s presence was slim. The official results list only Evie Palmer, 18, who placed 24th in the women’s 16 and over 50m breaststroke heats in 35.36 and did not reach the final. The club had identified Palmer and a swimmer named Jacob as its regional qualifiers; no Tavistock swimmer named Jacob appears in the result sheets.

Host club Millfield and Mount Kelly dominated the wider medal list. Compiled from official finals and timed finals, Mount Kelly recorded 33 event wins, 41 silvers and 32 bronzes among 101 swimmers who appear in the results, and Millfield 19 golds, 12 silvers and 9 bronzes among 74 swimmers. Jasmine Hall, 16, won four events for Mount Kelly, including the 16-year-old 400m individual medley in 4:48.43, the 16 and over 200m freestyle in 2:00.06, the 200m individual medley in 2:16.34 and the 400m freestyle in 4:15.02. Joey Bale, 15, completed a 13–15 breaststroke treble (50m 28.73, 100m 1:03.08, 200m 2:18.40), and Jayden Carter, 15, won the 13–15 50m freestyle (23.56), 100m freestyle (52.97) and 50m butterfly (24.75). Zerina Vrabac, 16, won the 16-year-old 1500m freestyle in 17:22.36 and Amelia Wright, 16, the 16 and over 100m freestyle in 55.78. Millfield’s Wilfred Leigh, 17, won the 16 and over 100m freestyle (50.66), 100m breaststroke (1:02.77) and 100m individual medley (56.59). Charlie Phipps, 15, took the 13–15 200m freestyle (1:46.46), 200m backstroke (1:57.42) and 200m individual medley (2:05.29). Kaden Frampton, 16, won the 16-year-old 1500m freestyle in 16:04.57, and Ernest Darkin, 17, the 17 and over 1500m in 16:07.49.

The next major national target is the Swim England National Winter Championships (25m) at Ponds Forge, Sheffield, from 10–13 December. Closer to home, Plymouth Leander’s Christmas Level 3 Open Meet at Plymouth Life Centre — listed by the host club for 7, 13 and 14 December, and described as a last-chance qualifier for county championships — is the next local meet to follow, with Devon County Championships due from 23 January to 6 February 2027 and Cornwall’s age-group championships listed by Caradon for 31 January and 7 February 2027.
