# McSharry Wins 100 Breast and Medals in All Three Breaststroke Events as Walshe Takes 200 Fly Silver at Baku World Cup

*Baku, Azerbaijan — Oct. 3, 2026*

Mona McSharry won a medal in each of her three events at the opening stop of the 2026 World Aquatics Swimming World Cup, Oct. 1–3 at the Baku Aquatic Palace. She won the 100m breaststroke, took silver in the 200 and bronze in the 50. Ellen Walshe added silver in the 200 butterfly, giving Ireland four medals from the short-course (25m) meet.

McSharry, the 26-year-old Olympic bronze medallist from Co. Sligo who trains at the University of Tennessee, and Walshe, 25, of Templeogue SC, were Ireland’s only entries.

McSharry led the 100 breast heats in 1:04.94 and was faster again in the final, winning in 1:04.21. She was second at the 50 in 30.35, 0.04 behind South Africa’s Aimee Canny, then split 16.85 and 17.01 over the last two lengths to win by 0.92 seconds. Russia’s Evgeniia Chikunova was second in 1:05.13 and Canny third in 1:05.16. It was the second World Cup win of McSharry’s career, after the 50 breast at the 2025 Westmont stop, and 0.37 outside her Irish record of 1:03.84 from the 2025 series.

The meet opened on Thursday with silver for McSharry in the 200 breast in 2:18.45, 0.18 outside the Irish record she set at the 2025 World Cup. She was second at every 25 from the 50 on. Chikunova won in 2:17.20, and Vitalina Simonova was third in 2:21.21.

On the final day, McSharry qualified third for the 50 breast in 30.05, then took bronze in 29.83, 0.08 behind Belgium’s Florine Gaspard (29.75) and 0.02 behind Russia’s Ralina Giliazova (29.81).

Walshe also opened with a medal, taking silver in the 200 fly in 2:05.58. China’s Mohan Chang won in 2:04.15, and Xinyang Zhou took bronze in 2:05.65, 0.07 behind Walshe. Walshe went into the meet with an entry time of 2:02.36.

Walshe was fifth in the 400 IM in 4:29.13, 0.37 off bronze and 0.16 behind Japan’s Waka Kobori (4:28.97). China’s Yiting Yu won in 4:24.09 ahead of Great Britain’s Abbie Wood (4:27.95) and Chang (4:28.76). Walshe closed her meet with fifth in the 200 IM in 2:08.07; Yu won that race in 2:02.80, ahead of the Netherlands’ Marrit Steenbergen (2:05.09) and Wood (2:05.69).

Both swimmers stay on the circuit for the second stop in Tashkent, Uzbekistan, Oct. 8–10, before the series ends in Astana, Kazakhstan, Oct. 15–17.
