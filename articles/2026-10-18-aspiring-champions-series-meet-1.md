# Dylan Registe Wins the 50 Free and 50 Fly as Lisburn Takes 18 Medals at Aspiring Champions Meet 1

*Belfast, Northern Ireland — Oct. 18, 2026*

Lisburn City Swimming Club collected 18 age-group medals at Swim Ulster’s Aspiring Champions Series Meet 1, Oct. 16–18 at Lisnasharragh Leisure Centre in Belfast. Forty-eight club swimmers contested the short-course meet, with Dylan Registe winning twice in the men’s 17-and-over sprints and Eoghan McQuillan taking the 13–14 100 backstroke.

Registe, 21, won the 17-and-over 50 free in 22.47, 0.07 ahead of Bangor’s Oliver Watts (22.54), and the 17-and-over 50 fly in 23.95, more than a second clear of Bangor’s Reuben Agbaje (25.15). He was second in the 17-and-over 100 IM in 57.54, behind Bangor’s Calvin Fry (55.47) and just ahead of Swim Belfast’s Brandon Biss (57.87), and second in the 17-and-over 50 back in 26.94, with Biss winning in 26.10.

McQuillan, 14, won the men’s 13–14 100 back in 1:04.40 and added silver in the 13–14 200 free in 2:04.09. He also took bronze in four 13–14 events: the 100 free (57.65), 100 fly (1:03.49), 50 back (30.26) and 100 breaststroke (1:14.61).
