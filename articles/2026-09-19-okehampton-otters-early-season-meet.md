# Tavistock Collect 47 Medals at Okehampton Otters Early Season Meet

*Okehampton — 19 September 2026*

Tavistock Swimming Club made a strong start to the short-course season at the Okehampton Otters Low Level 4 Early Season Meet, a sprint gala at Parklands Leisure Centre in Simmons Park on 19 September. Session 1 was for 8–11 year-olds and session 2 for 12 and over, with 50m strokes, 100m freestyle and 100m individual medley on the programme and medals to the top three in each age category.

Tavistock, listed as TAVW in the official results, was the only one of the region’s priority clubs to appear in the result sheets. The 22-swimmer team collected 15 gold, 15 silver and 17 bronze medals in the official timed finals.

Lily Shutt, 13, won three events, the 50m freestyle (34.33), 100m freestyle (1:16.22) and 50m breaststroke (47.48), and added silver in the 100m individual medley (1:28.15) and 50m butterfly (41.62) and bronze in the 50m backstroke (45.43). Lottie Squire, listed as Lottie V Squire, 12, won the 50m freestyle (32.57) and 100m freestyle (1:09.26) and was second in the 100m individual medley (1:23.38), 50m backstroke (42.14) and 50m butterfly (39.36).

Among the younger age groups, Rosie Warren-Green, 10, won the 100m individual medley in 1:43.26 and the 50m backstroke in 43.21, with bronze in the 100m freestyle and 50m breaststroke. Sophia Brayne, 11, won the 100m individual medley (1:39.02) and 50m breaststroke (52.29) and took three further bronzes. Scarlett Blackmore, 11, won the 50m backstroke in 46.69 and finished with six medals in all. Nell Balment, 10, won the 100m freestyle in 1:29.01, Isabella Moon, 10, the 50m freestyle in 38.51, Sophia Lewis, 9, the 50m breaststroke in 59.64, and Dom Shearman, listed as Dom V Shearman, 10, the 50m butterfly in 50.68.

In the older session, Eloise Patrick, 15, won the 50m breaststroke in 46.83 and was second in the 100m individual medley and 50m backstroke. Cora George, 13, took four medals, with silver in the 50m backstroke and 50m freestyle. Alleyanna Campbell, 14, was second in the 50m freestyle, and Illithia Smerdon and Keeley Campbell also reached the age-group podium, as did Harris Barclay, 8, in the younger session.

Plymouth College Aquatics, Plymouth Leander, Devonport Royal, Caradon, Millfield and Mount Kelly did not appear in the official results. Tavistock raced alongside the host Okehampton Otters and visiting clubs including Exeter City, Launceston, Brixham, Crediton and Torridgeside.
