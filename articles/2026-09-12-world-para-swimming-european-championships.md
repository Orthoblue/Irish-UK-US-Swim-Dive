# Registe Finishes Fourth in the SB14 100 Breaststroke at the World Para Swimming European Championships

*Kocaeli, Turkey — Sept. 12, 2026*

Lisburn City Swimming Club’s Deaten Registe opened the 2026–27 season with a fourth-place finish in the men’s SB14 100 breaststroke at the World Para Swimming European Championships, Sept. 7–12 in Kocaeli, Turkey. The Paris 2024 Paralympian was 0.29 seconds from a medal in the final.

Registe, 23, competing for Ireland, swam the event on Sept. 9. He qualified fourth from the heats in 1:08.44, then went 1:08.36 in the evening final, a 0.08-second improvement.

Great Britain’s Quinn-Austin Kelly won in 1:04.96, with Germany’s Florian Reiter second in 1:07.63. Ukraine’s Vasyl Krainyk took bronze in 1:08.07. Registe was fourth in 1:08.36, 0.11 ahead of Iceland’s Robert Isak Jonsson (1:08.47). Belgium’s Aymeric Parmentier was sixth in 1:09.44.

In the heats, official results listed Registe in 1:08.44 with a 32.05 split at 50 meters. Paralympics Ireland reported that he qualified as the fourth-fastest swimmer, 0.01 behind Reiter. In the final he split 31.90 and 36.46, with a reaction time of 0.62, and finished 3.40 seconds behind Kelly.

The same evening brought Ireland’s first medals of the championships, with Róisín Ní Riain second in the women’s S13 100 free and Dearbhaile Brady second in the women’s SM6 200 IM, Paralympics Ireland and BBC Sport reported.

After the final, Registe told Paralympics Ireland: “I feel good. It was better than this morning, that’s all I can ask for. I probably could have paced the second 50 a bit better, but apart from that I can’t be too upset. It’s faster than this morning, so I’m happy enough.”

The official results book lists no other individual swims for Registe at these championships.

Lisburn’s domestic season continues at Swim Ulster’s Aspiring Champions Series Meet 1, Oct. 16–18, at Lisnasharragh Leisure Centre in Belfast.
