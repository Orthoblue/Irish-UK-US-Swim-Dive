# Nic Brádaigh Makes Three Finals and Registe Places Fourth at the World Para Swimming European Championships

*Kocaeli, Turkey — Sept. 12, 2026*

Two Lisburn City Swimming Club athletes competed for Ireland at the World Para Swimming European Championships, Sept. 7–12 in Kocaeli, Turkey. Síomha Nic Brádaigh reached three finals, placing fifth in the women’s SM7 200 IM, and Deaten Registe finished fourth in the men’s SB14 100 breaststroke, 0.29 seconds from a medal. Paralympics Ireland listed both as Lisburn City swimmers, coached by Stan Sheppard, on the official team announcement.

Nic Brádaigh, making her European Championship debut after racing at the 2025 World Championships, opened the meet on Sept. 7 with fifth place in the SM7 200 IM final in 3:27.78. She later placed sixth in the S7 100 backstroke and sixth in the S7 100 freestyle.

Ukraine’s Veronika Korzhova won the 200 IM in 2:58.04, with Russia’s Milana Shchelokova second in 3:09.71 and Ukraine’s Anna Hontar third in 3:10.16. Nic Brádaigh split 41.81, 1:32.58 and 2:36.67 on the way to 3:27.78, 7.58 behind Spain’s Leyre Orti Campos in fourth. After the final she told Paralympics Ireland: “We’ve had such a long season so that swim, for me, was nearly a relief to get in and get at it. I’m a wee bit off my PB, so I’m a little bit disappointed, but it’s a massive relief to get in and race.”

On Sept. 10 she qualified fifth for the S7 100 back final in 1:33.93, then was sixth in the evening final in 1:34.00. Korzhova won in a world record 1:19.35. Official results listed Nic Brádaigh 14.65 seconds back, with a 44.57 split at 50 meters.

She closed her week on Sept. 11 in the S7 100 free, qualifying seventh in 1:23.43 and placing sixth in the final in 1:23.20 after a 38.99 opening 50. Korzhova won in 1:12.02. Russia’s Anna Bogatyreva and Hontar tied for second in 1:13.86.

Registe, a Paris 2024 Paralympian, swam the SB14 100 breaststroke on Sept. 9. He qualified fourth from the heats in 1:08.44, then went 1:08.36 in the evening final, a 0.08-second improvement. Great Britain’s Quinn-Austin Kelly won in 1:04.96, with Germany’s Florian Reiter second in 1:07.63. Ukraine’s Vasyl Krainyk took bronze in 1:08.07. Registe was fourth, 0.11 ahead of Iceland’s Robert Isak Jonsson (1:08.47). In the final he split 31.90 and 36.46, with a reaction time of 0.62, and finished 3.40 seconds behind Kelly.

The same evening brought Ireland’s first medals of the championships, with Róisín Ní Riain second in the women’s S13 100 free and Dearbhaile Brady second in the women’s SM6 200 IM, Paralympics Ireland and BBC Sport reported.

After the final, Registe told Paralympics Ireland: “I feel good. It was better than this morning, that’s all I can ask for. I probably could have paced the second 50 a bit better, but apart from that I can’t be too upset. It’s faster than this morning, so I’m happy enough.”

The official results book lists no other individual swims for either Lisburn swimmer at these championships.

Lisburn’s domestic season continues at Swim Ulster’s Aspiring Champions Series Meet 1, Oct. 16–18, at Lisnasharragh Leisure Centre in Belfast.
