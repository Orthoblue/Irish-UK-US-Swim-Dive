# Lisburn Wins the 14-and-Under Medley Relay and Takes Silver in the Open Medley at the War Memorial Gala

*Bangor, Northern Ireland — Nov. 7, 2026*

Lisburn City Swimming Club left the War Memorial Gala with a gold medal and a silver on long course, Nov. 7 at the Aurora Aquatic and Leisure Complex in Bangor. The meet was a relay gala, and Lisburn’s men’s 14-and-under 200 medley relay won its final while the open 200 medley relay was second.

The 14-and-under medley relay of Eoghan McQuillan, Oskar Purlys, Harry Gray and James Wilson won in 2:02.01 after leading the prelims in 2:01.47 (McQuillan 31.08, Purlys 33.67, Gray 30.87, Wilson 25.85). In the final they split 31.26, 33.89, 30.63 and 26.23. Lisburn’s B-relay of Sam Bowman, Alexandre Kiliokaitis, Jackson Cosgrove and Nicolas Carlin was eighth in 2:19.48.

The open 200 medley relay of Adam Colgan, Deaten Registe, Dylan Registe and Alfie Shawe took silver in 1:45.43, behind Bangor (1:43.02). Colgan led off in 27.82, with Deaten Registe at 30.30, Dylan Registe at 23.65 and Shawe at 23.66. They had qualified third in 1:50.31. Lisburn’s B-relay of Ben McCrum, Ross Gray, Ben Gracey and Christopher Mason qualified eighth in 1:56.88 but was disqualified in the final. The C-relay of Noah McCaw, Neitas Sukauskas, Nelas Sukauskas and Jay Gallagher was 16th in prelims in 2:16.47.

The men’s 10-and-over 300 freestyle relay was fourth in 2:28.17, with a named lineup of Colgan, Christopher Mason, Ross Gray and Noah McCaw. Bangor won in 2:20.30, with Banbridge second in 2:27.41 and Ards third in 2:27.64. Lisburn had qualified fourth in 2:34.41. A B-relay was disqualified in the final.

The women’s open 200 medley relay of Aoife Mason, Amelia Stone, Emilee Edgar and Annabel McKeown placed eighth in 2:14.09 after qualifying in 2:11.59. Bangor won in 2:00.28. Lisburn’s B- and C-relays were 17th and 22nd in prelims. The open 200 free relay of Mason, McKeown, Edgar and Stone was 12th in 1:59.03.

The women’s 14-and-under 200 medley relay of Eimear Tian, Jade Elliott, Helen Cox and Emily Davey was sixth in 2:19.77. The 12-and-under 200 free relay of Abbie Brown, Gracie Kelly, Milana Flederiene and Tilly Lawlor was seventh in 2:19.33 after a 2:17.78 prelim. The men’s 12-and-under 200 free relay of Jamie Gracey, Sam Gray, Eunan McCrudden and Myles McMullan qualified second in 2:09.44 but was disqualified in the final.

Lisburn is next at the Swim Ulster Short Course Championships, Nov. 20–22, at Lagan Valley Leisureplex.
