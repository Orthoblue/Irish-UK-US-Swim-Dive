# Edgar Takes 200 Fly Silver as Lisburn Claims Six Medals at Ulster Short Course Championships

*Lisburn, Northern Ireland — Nov. 22, 2026*

Lisburn City Swimming Club left its home pool with six medals at the Swim Ulster Short Course Championships, Nov. 20–22 at Lagan Valley Leisureplex. Twenty-two club swimmers competed in the provincial short-course championships, picking up one silver and five bronzes across individual events and a relay.

Emilee Edgar, 18, took silver in the women's 12-and-over 200 butterfly. Aoife Mason, Helen Cox, Deaten Registe and Eoghan McQuillan each won individual bronze, and the men's 200 medley relay was third.

**Lisburn medals** (official results)

| Place | Event | Swimmer / relay | Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2nd | W 200 butterfly | Emilee Edgar, 18 | 2:26.39 |
| 3rd | W 200 backstroke | Aoife Mason, 17 | 2:24.60 |
| 3rd | W 800 free | Helen Cox, 14 | 9:41.00 |
| 3rd | M 50 breaststroke | Deaten Registe, 23 | 30.13 |
| 3rd | Mixed 1,500 free | Eoghan McQuillan, 14 | 17:19.46 |
| 3rd | M 200 medley relay | McCrum, Registe, Wilson, Gracey | 1:52.45 |

Edgar led the 200 butterfly prelims in 2:30.93, more than six seconds clear of the field, then dropped to 2:26.39 in the final. Banbridge's Leah Jacobs, fifth in prelims in 2:46.33, won in 2:25.93, 0.46 ahead of Edgar. Bangor's Vanessa Hamilton was third in 2:33.98. Edgar also made the 50 fly B-final, placing 16th in 31.72.

Mason, 17, earned bronze in the women's 12-and-over 200 backstroke in 2:24.60 after a 2:27.09 prelim. She was sixth in the 100 back in 1:08.81 (prelim 1:08.22), eighth in the 50 back in 32.46 and 16th in the 50 free B-final in 28.89.

Cox, 14, was third in the women's 12-and-over 800 free in 9:41.00. She also placed sixth in the 12–16 400 free in 4:43.73, ninth in the 400 IM in 5:28.64, 12th in the mixed 1,500 free in 18:58.33 and 15th in the 200 free B-final in 2:15.62.

McQuillan, 14, took bronze in the mixed 12-and-over 1,500 free in 17:19.46, with clubmate Oskar Purlys, also 14, fourth in 17:24.77. McQuillan added sixth in the men's 400 free (4:22.62), sixth in the 200 back (2:14.66), seventh in the 200 IM (2:17.51), fifth in the 400 IM (4:52.31) and sixth in the 800 free (9:04.42). Purlys was fifth in the 800 free in 9:03.72 and sixth in the 400 IM in 4:54.90. Sam Bowman, 14, was ninth in the 800 free (9:41.02) and 10th in the mixed 1,500 (18:23.27). Nancy Craig, 15, and Lilia Bowman, 13, also contested the women's 800 and the mixed 1,500.

Registe, 23, a Paris 2024 Paralympian in the SB14 100 breaststroke, won bronze in the men's 12-and-over 50 breaststroke in 30.13 after qualifying ninth in 30.85. He was fourth in the 100 breast in 1:06.74. Ross Gray, 16, went 5-5-7 in the 200, 100 and 50 breaststroke (2:32.60, 1:07.02, 30.56) and was eighth in the 200 IM in 2:18.40.

The men's 200 medley relay took bronze in 1:52.45 behind Banbridge (1:44.23) and Lurgan (1:49.59). Ben McCrum (28.81), Registe (29.93), James Wilson (27.24) and Ben Gracey (26.47) swam the four strokes. Lisburn's B-relay of McQuillan (29.00), Gray (31.17), Cian Garland (28.41) and Purlys (26.46) was seventh in 1:55.04.

The women were fifth in the 200 medley relay in 2:08.40 (Mason 32.11, Emma Bradley 37.27, Edgar 31.71, Amelia Stone 27.31) and eighth in the 200 free relay in 1:57.96 (Mason 30.20, Stone 28.04, Cox 30.02, Bradley 29.70). The men's 200 free relay of Wilson (26.29), McCrum (25.32), Garland (27.48) and Gracey (26.71) was seventh in 1:45.80. A men's B free relay was disqualified.

Stone, 16, qualified third in 50 breaststroke prelims in 35.78 and was fifth in the final in 35.39. She also qualified fourth in 50 free prelims in 27.94. McCrum, 16, was seventh in the 50 back in 29.08 and eighth in the 50 fly in 28.09. Wilson, 14, was 11th in the 50 fly in 27.73 and 13th in the 100 fly in 1:03.21. Garland, 17, took seventh in the 200 fly in 2:26.97. Erin Fitzpatrick, 17, and Isabella Kiliokaitis, 17, were fourth and fifth in the women's 17–18 400 free in 5:01.76 and 5:09.06.

The Irish Winter National Championships (25m) are next on the national calendar, Dec. 18–20 at the National Aquatic Centre in Dublin. Qualification for that meet closed at the end of these championships.
