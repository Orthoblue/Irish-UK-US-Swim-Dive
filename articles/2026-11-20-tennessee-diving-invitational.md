# Hubred, Landi and Hallifax Podium as Auburn Divers Finish Fourth at Tennessee Invitational

*Knoxville, Tenn. — Nov. 20, 2026*

Auburn’s divers came away with three podium finishes at the Tennessee Invitational, Nov. 18–20, at the Allan Jones Intercollegiate Aquatic Center, while the Tiger swimmers were at the Wolfpack Elite Invitational in Greensboro.

The official results are for a full invitational that also included swimming. Auburn entered only divers and still placed fourth on both sides: the women scored 123 points, the men 124. Tennessee won both team races (women 1,288, men 1,138.5), ahead of Virginia and North Carolina. Indiana, Alabama and George Washington rounded out the seven-team field.

Freshman Peder Hubred opened the meet with a bronze on men’s 3-meter. He led Auburn in prelims at 405.10, a score official coverage called the seventh-best by an Auburn performer, and took third in the final at 396.95 behind Tennessee’s Thomas Ciprik (457.20) and Bennett Greene (417.75). Ethan Swart was fifth in 381.25, Talan Blackmon seventh in 368.30 and Benjamin Wilson 10th in 350.50.

Juliette Landi was third on women’s 3-meter the next day in 352.70. Official coverage called that the 10th-best performance and fifth-best performer in Auburn history. Chloe Brothers was sixth in 329.10, Abigail Suits eighth in 303.05 and June Sundheim 11th in 291.95. Maria Faoro was 16th in prelims (258.00) and did not advance.

On 1-meter, Swart was fourth in 364.80 and Blackmon eighth in 343.10. Brothers was eighth on women’s 1-meter in 278.35, and Faoro was ninth in 273.15. Emily Hallifax and Landi were entered but did not start the 1-meter final (official results: SCR).

Hallifax took silver on women’s platform with 285.95 on five dives, behind North Carolina’s Sofia Knight (314.20). Brothers was fifth (239.65), Sundheim eighth (227.50), Suits 11th (195.95) and Landi 12th (187.45). Swart led the Auburn men on platform with a fifth-place 362.25 on six dives. Tsvetomir Ereminov was sixth (351.30), Hubred ninth (300.45), Wilson 10th (293.50) and Blackmon 11th (278.75).
