# Machuco Wins Three Events as Auburn Men Beat LSU; Brothers Sweeps Women’s Diving

*Baton Rouge, La. — Nov. 5, 2026*

The 14th-ranked Auburn men beat 18th-ranked LSU 163–135 at the LSU Natatorium, and the 19th-ranked Tiger women fell 120.5–179.5 to the 17th-ranked Tigers. The combined score was 283.5–314.5 for the hosts. (Rankings are from Auburn’s official recap.)

Gabriel Machuco won three individual events. He took the 200 free in 1:34.38, a U.S. Open cut, ahead of teammate Daniel Krichevsky (1:34.79, also a U.S. Open cut). He won the 200 back in 1:43.28 and the 200 IM in 1:44.03. Maston Ballew was third in the 200 IM in 1:48.30.

Lora Komoroczy won the 100 back in 51.23, an NCAA and U.S. Open cut that official coverage called the fifth-fastest time in Auburn history and her ninth different mark on the event’s top-25 list. She was second in the 100 fly in 52.84. Ellis Fox was third in the 100 back in 52.93, a U.S. Open cut, and third in the 200 back in 1:57.07; Maggie McGuire was second in the 200 back in 1:56.21.

Zetta Grace Bartee won the 200 fly in 1:57.10, an NCAA and U.S. Open cut, and Auburn went 1-2-3-4: Coco Croxford 1:58.75, Taylor Bacher 2:00.19 and Morgan Carteaux 2:00.40. Luke Bedsole won the men’s 200 fly in 1:45.42. Bradford Johnson was second in the 100 fly in 45.42, an NCAA and U.S. Open cut that official coverage called the 18th-fastest time and eighth-fastest performer in program history.

The men closed the meet by winning the 400 free relay in 2:50.02 (Alexey Glivinsky 43.03, Warner Russ 42.58, Mike Rice 42.46, freshman Matthew Hamilton 41.95). They were second in the 200 medley relay in 1:24.19 (Bedsole 21.77, Uros Zivanovic 23.15, Johnson 20.43, Russ 18.84). Russ was second in the 50 free (19.35) and 100 free (43.06); Glivinsky was third in the 100 free in 43.15; Rice was fourth in 43.36. Zivanovic and Kaua Carvalho went 2-3 in the 100 breast (52.33, 52.65) and 2-3 in the 200 breast (1:57.63, 1:58.27). Ivan Tarasov (47.10) and Sebestyen Papp (47.23) were second and third in the 100 back. Nora Weber was second in the 1,000 free in 10:01.66. Zoey Zeller was third in the 200 breast in 2:09.81 and third in the 200 IM in 2:00.51. Rosalie Reef was third in the 100 free in 48.70.

Auburn’s divers swept both women’s boards and both men’s boards. Chloe Brothers won the 1-meter (299.70) and 3-meter (342.15); official coverage said the 1-meter score made her the ninth-best performer in program history and the 3-meter score ranked seventh all-time. Emily Hallifax (299.33), Maria Faoro (292.95), June Sundheim (279.38) and Abigail Suits (262.73) completed a 1-through-5 finish on the women’s 1-meter. Ethan Swart won the men’s 1-meter in 373.20, which official coverage said tied Conner Pruitt (January 2023) for the 20th-best performance in program history, and won the 3-meter in 400.35. Talan Blackmon was second on 1-meter (359.48) and third on 3-meter (377.63). Peder Hubred was second on 3-meter (390.15). Benjamin Wilson was top-four on both boards (1-meter 334.05, 3-meter 377.33).

Hamilton was third in the 50 free in 19.39, a U.S. Open cut, and third in the 100 fly in 46.19, another U.S. Open cut and 0.70 faster than his 46.89 from CSL Match 6. He split 20.65 fly on Auburn’s B 200 medley relay, third in 1:25.13 (Tarasov 21.66, Carvalho 23.69, Hamilton 20.65, Nicolas Gama 19.13), and anchored the winning 400 free relay in 41.95.
