Reference

Useful links

Official and widely used sites for swimming and diving in the United States, the United Kingdom, and Ireland, with Auburn in mind. Each address was checked on 27 November 2026.

National rankings

Where to look up times and ranking lists.

  • Swim England rankings, last 12 months (opens in a new tab)

    Ranked long-course and short-course swims from the past year, on the British swimming results service.

  • Swim England all-time rankings (opens in a new tab)

    All-time event rankings for British swimming, long course and short course.

  • Individual best times (opens in a new tab)

    Look up a swimmer’s personal bests for the last 12 months or all time.

  • Swim Ireland records, rankings and results (opens in a new tab)

    Swim Ireland’s section for national records, rankings, and meet results.

  • Swim Ulster results (opens in a new tab)

    Published results from Swim Ulster meets.

  • USA Swimming times (opens in a new tab)

    USA Swimming’s times section, including rankings, records, and meet results.

  • USA Swimming Data Hub (opens in a new tab)

    The current hub for USA Swimming athlete data, rankings, and results. It replaces the older SWIMS lookup.

  • SwimCloud (opens in a new tab)

    College and club times, NCAA-style rankings, and live results used widely in the US.

Schools

England has its own schools association. Irish and Ulster school championships are run through Swim Ireland and Swim Ulster; no separate schools site could be confirmed.

  • English Schools’ Swimming Association (opens in a new tab)

    ESSA, the body for school swimming in England, including team championships and water polo.

  • ESSA national calendar (opens in a new tab)

    The standing calendar of ESSA national events.

  • ESSA Secondary Schools’ Team Championships (opens in a new tab)

    The national secondary schools team championship, with qualifying rounds and the final.

  • ESSA Primary Team Championships (opens in a new tab)

    The national primary schools team championship.

  • Swim Ireland competition calendar (opens in a new tab)

    National, provincial, and schools meets on Swim Ireland’s calendar.

  • Swim Ulster events (opens in a new tab)

    Provincial meets in Ulster, including school competition published by Swim Ulster.

National federations, college swimming, and the places fans actually open for meet results.

    Swim England’s hub for clubs, competition, and the performance pathway.

  • Swim England calendar (opens in a new tab)

    Licensed meets and events across Swim England disciplines.

  • Aquatics GB (opens in a new tab)

  • Aquatics GB rankings, records and results (opens in a new tab)

    British national and international rankings, records, and results.

  • Swim Wales (opens in a new tab)

  • Scottish Swimming competitions (opens in a new tab)

  • Swim Ireland competitions and events (opens in a new tab)

    National championships, regulations, and the Irish competition programme.

  • Swim Ulster (opens in a new tab)

    The provincial body for swimming in Northern Ireland and the Ulster counties.

  • BUCS swimming (opens in a new tab)

    British Universities and Colleges Sport swimming, including the championships programme.

  • NCAA Division I men’s swimming and diving (opens in a new tab)

    The NCAA page for the Division I men’s swimming and diving championship.

  • NCAA Division I women’s swimming and diving (opens in a new tab)

    The NCAA page for the Division I women’s swimming and diving championship.

  • Southeastern Conference (opens in a new tab)

    SEC home, where conference swimming and diving news and championship coverage is published.

  • Auburn swimming and diving (opens in a new tab)

    Auburn University’s swimming and diving site: roster, schedule, and news.

  • USA Swimming events (opens in a new tab)

    National meets and the USA Swimming event calendar.

  • British meet results (opens in a new tab)

    Search published meet results on swimmingresults.org, by meet, club, or event.

  • Meet Mobile (opens in a new tab)

    The Hy-Tek app fans use for live times and results at meets in the US and elsewhere.

  • Hy-Tek (opens in a new tab)

    Home of Meet Manager and Team Manager, the software behind most official meet files.

  • SwimResults live results (opens in a new tab)

    A live-results site linked from Swim Ireland for meets as they happen.
