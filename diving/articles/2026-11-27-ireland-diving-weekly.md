# Emma Kelly Named Swim Ireland Pathway Athlete of the Year, Then Makes Two Finals in Knoxville

*Dublin, Ireland — Nov. 27, 2026*

Emma Kelly was the main Irish diving story of November. At the 2026 Swim Ireland Awards on Nov. 14 at the Carlton Hotel, Blanchardstown, she was named Female Performance Pathway Athlete of the Year. Swim Ireland lists her club as Limerick SC and said she reached a European Junior final and finished in the top 25 at the World Junior Championships this season. She was the only diver among the night's winners.

Kelly is now a freshman diver at the University of North Carolina. UNC said in August that its "diving newcomer Emma Kelly represented Ireland at the World Aquatics Junior Championships, finishing in the Top 25 in both the 1-meter and 3-meter competitions."

Four days after the awards, she was in the Tar Heels' line-up at the Tennessee Invitational in Knoxville (Nov. 17–20). On women's 1-meter she qualified 10th in 252.05 and climbed to fifth in the A-final with 288.95. On 3-meter she qualified 11th (281.85) and finished 10th in the final (292.15). She qualified ninth on platform (226.60), but the official results do not list her among the 12 divers in the final.
